## 2. Country Coverage Metrics

### Before Expansion (Baseline)
* **Total Covered Countries**: 8 countries (Japan, UK, US, France, Germany, Italy, Qatar, Philippines, Peru)
* **Uncovered Geographic Regions**: 12 out of 15 major world regions had 0 or 1 news sources.

### After Proposed Initial Expansion (37 Feeds Selected)
* **Total Covered Countries**: 32 countries (India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Vietnam, Myanmar, Cambodia, Philippines, China, Hong Kong, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand/Pacific, Papua New Guinea, Tonga, Poland, Romania, Croatia, Regional Balkans, Spain, Sweden, Israel, Middle East Region, Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, Zambia, DR Congo, Mexico, Guatemala, Panama, Cuba, Jamaica, Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Venezuela, US)
* **Geographic Coverage Expansion**: From 8 countries to 32 countries (+300% country diversity).

## 3. Targeted Gap Resolutions

### A. South Asia & Southeast Asia
* **Problem**: 2.5 billion population represented by only 1 publisher (Manila Bulletin).
* **Resolution**: Added 4 South Asian feeds (The Hindu, Indian Express [IN], Dawn [PK], The Daily Star [BD]) and 7 Southeast Asian feeds (ANTARA [ID], Malay Mail [MY], The Straits Times [SG], VnExpress [VN], Myanmar Now [MM], Khmer Times [KH], Inquirer [PH]).

### B. Sub-Saharan Africa
* **Problem**: Zero coverage across all 54 African nations.
* **Resolution**: Established multi-anchor African coverage across West Africa (Vanguard [NG], JoyOnline [GH]), East Africa (The Standard [KE]), Southern Africa (Times of Zambia [ZM]), and Central Africa (Radio Okapi [CD]).

### C. Latin America & Caribbean
* **Problem**: Only 1 source (El Comercio Peru).
* **Resolution**: Added 5 Central America/Caribbean feeds (El Universal [MX], Prensa Libre [GT], Newsroom Panama [PA], Granma [CU], Jamaica Gleaner [JM]) and 4 South America feeds (Folha de S.Paulo [BR], Buenos Aires Times [AR], El Tiempo [CO], El Nacional [VE]).

### D. Eastern Europe & Balkans
* **Problem**: Zero coverage despite ongoing regional conflicts and geopolitical significance.
* **Resolution**: Added Notes from Poland (PL), Romania Insider (RO), Total Croatia News (HR), and Balkan Insight (Regional Balkans).

### E. Oceania & Pacific Islands
* **Problem**: Zero coverage for Australia, NZ, and Pacific island nations.
* **Resolution**: Added ABC News Australia (AU), RNZ Pacific (NZ/Pacific), PNG Post-Courier (PG), and Matangi Tonga (TO).
